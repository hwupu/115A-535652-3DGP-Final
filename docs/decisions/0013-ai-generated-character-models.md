# 0013 — AI-generated character models (Tripo3D), rigged and animated by script
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03
- Deciders: Henry (PO, art owner) with Claude

## Context
The game needs three characters: the hamster hero, the hamster princess and Sir Grumblepaws (L1 cat). #36 asks for Blender clips driven by an Animator. Henry generated the three models with Tripo3D AI (chibi, 3-head-tall, T-pose; free tier, CC BY 4.0). They come unrigged, 50k–500k triangles, with 4K textures.

## Decision
- Use the Tripo3D models for the characters, credited in `CREDITS.md` / `docs/assets.md` as CC BY 4.0, generated by Henry with Tripo3D.
- Process them in Blender **by script** (`Art/Characters/BlenderScripts~`): decimate to 15k triangles, 2K base colour only, a simple Generic biped rig with automatic weights, clips keyed in code. Reproducible and small (~3 MB per character in LFS).
- One Magic tool (*Magic > Prefabs > Use Character Models*) sets the import, material and Animator controller and swaps the models into the existing `Prefabs/Characters` prefabs; `GolferAvatar` and `GardenCat` drive the Animators.

## Consequences
- AI-generated models are **not manual artwork**: #80 (Form B, >= 1 hand-made model per member) still needs hand-made models (e.g. the three tools). The report must say these are AI-generated.
- The placeholder looks and their code fallbacks stay for prefabs without an Animator; mini-exercises don't use these prefabs.
- Pose tweaks mean editing `anim.py` and re-exporting (or editing the `.blend` by hand and exporting with the documented settings).
